## High-priority finding: cold direct activation

HAL.h:38 stepperEnableDelayMs=25, delayMilliseconds calls Arduino delay(). Printer.h:296–331 enableX/Y/ZStepper and Extruder.h:301–319 enable() write enable and on first activation call this delay. HAL.cpp Timer1 masks OCIE1A and enters performDirectMove with I clear. StepExecutor.cpp:315–336 DIRECT_PREPARED[_STOPPABLE] branch sets running phase and calls enableSteppers without first opening I, unlike queued activation at :218. Printer::offsetRelativeStepsCoordinates and motion.cpp::prepareDirectMove reserve and publish direct move without enabling axes first. A cold direct move is thus a plausible/reachable source-level path to delay(25) with I clear. 25 ms nominal requested delay = 400000 cycles, far beyond 400/512 cycles; actual masked duration is NOT 400000 measured cycles: Arduino micros depends on Timer0 overflow servicing and can misbehave without it. Prior diagnostic failures in micros() are not proof of actual hang. Warm activation bypasses enable wait; must distinguish these cases. Also cold queued activation may stall foreground for 25 ms per newly enabled axis with interrupts enabled, so heater authorization/foreground service and UART ring fill still matter. Do not remove electrical settling requirement blindly.

Candidate: move first-enable and bounded settling outside Timer1 to explicit foreground preparation before publish, or use nonblocking activation deadline/state; preserve stop/key-release, no STEP before settle, pause/direct ownership, and emergency abort/no-rearm. A mere sei before delay reduces blackout but not foreground stall and allows PWM fastAction to shorten a direct move, so not final recommendation. Check actual enable-time requirement with DRV worker; use existing configured 25 ms until authorized evidence. P0 owner alerted because safety priority; central owns final integration.

## Cold enable: historical provenance and electrical requirement (owner question)

Historical [changelog](https://github.com/Nibbels/Repetier-Firmware/blob/community_development/changelog.txt#L2232-L2239) explicitly introduces configurable STEPPER_ON_DELAY in V0.91.15 (2014-03-26), immediately after DRV8711 configuration changes for more precise stepping. No numerical justification or original measurement in that entry. Exact original 2014 numeric default and individual author not verified. Official Conrad [RF.01.38, 2017-12-19, pinned 0e52693eaed1f606af411e4898030b033304b94f](https://github.com/RF1000/Repetier-Firmware/blob/0e52693eaed1f606af411e4898030b033304b94f/RF1000/Repetier/Configuration.h) already defines 25 ms and explains avoiding premature initial steps, but no empirical derivation. Community RF1000.h/RF2000.h retain 25 ms; current HAL.h consolidates it. This predates the community work.

Actual board wiring checked visually against official Conrad schematics: [RF1000 I851 CTC V2.1, sheets 3–5](https://asset.conrad.com/media10/add/160267/c1/-/en/001007508CD01/circuit-diagramm-1007508-renkforce-rf1000-3d-printer.pdf), [RF2000 I851 CTC V3.0, sheets 3–5](https://asset.conrad.com/media10/add/160267/c1/-/en/001395717CD01/schema-electrique-1395717-renkforce-rf2000-imprimante-3d-appareil-pret-a-lemploi-banc-dimpression-chauffant.pdf) (correct RF2000 URL in docs/board-connectors.md). ENA_DRV1..5 connects directly to SLEEPn pin 8, not SPI CTRL.ENBL; RESET_DRV to RESET pin 9. Current pins.h maps ENA nets to X/Y/Z/E0/E1_ENABLE_PIN, active high in board profiles. Thus normal enable is actual wake from sleep, even though ENBL is configured separately. RF2000v2 exact driver circuit not independently verified; shared firmware pin map is not hardware proof.

[TI DRV8711 SLVSC40H](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/drv8711.pdf), p8 section 6.6: tSLEEP MAX 1 ms (confirmed from table screenshot, not MIN); tRESET serial-interface-ready MAX 10 us, distinct from full readiness. Section 7.4.1: approximately 1 ms after exit from RESET or SLEEP before full function/STEP respectively; sleep disables charge pump, gate regulator and analog circuitry, digital SPI registers remain accessible. p9 STEP high/low min 1.9 us, DIR/MODE setup/hold min 200 ns. p8 DAC response max 5 us is not winding current settling. No 25 ms electrical requirement found. Motor winding current rise and rotor/load settling are separate; cannot infer worst-case mechanical readiness from driver wake specification. No measured RF motor L/R/torque/load settling yet. No zero-wait proposal; 1 ms is electrical bound under specified conditions, not qualified system-level delay. Evaluate 2–5 ms candidates as conservative engineering hypotheses (not datasheet requirements or selected shipping defaults) only with later approval and physical evidence. First remove blocking waits via one wake batch and monotonic deadline; retain 25 ms policy during structural regression, then independent timing reduction. Gate ALL physical pulses, including Advance, standalone Z correction, scan/homing/manual and direct/queue; retain key-release, emergency stop/disable, startup and invalidation semantics. Do not serialize independent axis wake delays; enable needed axes once, wait concurrently, maintain service. Existing 25 ms must not become a global blackout even if retained. No production change, no hardware action.
