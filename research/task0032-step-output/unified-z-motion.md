## Actual legacy mechanism (target 9255f7b)

- BedMatrix.cpp recalculateZCompensation computes the target from *executed nominal* XY/Z, geometry/fade, manual+SenseOffset and Digit-Z. Publication pairs correctionTarget with ECMP ratio under one lock; watchdog requests recalculation roughly every16ms (foreground service dependent). A target can change after all 24 queue slots were planned.
- StepExecutor::stepSlowedZCompensation is a second step scheduler, not a PrintLine. Its static mass (0..15) and waitSteps=mass+stepsPerTimerCall count calls, not elapsed time. Called once per idle dispatch or per packed primary XY iteration without nominal Z. No call in pure E active path. Its effective speed and acceleration depend on packing, path rate and idle/active mode; massWeight is not a physical acceleration. ZMotion::stepCorrection owns direction, limits, physical motor and applied correction. ZMotion::stepPath cancels opposing correction against nominal Z, never blindly adds two incompatible pulses. Nominal Z and physical position remain separate.
- HAL Timer1 checks needCmpWait before admitting next move. correctionDelaysQueue uses a 0.25mm threshold plus a direction-dependent special rule on disable. This is a queue pacing hold, not an interrupt lock. It does not impose a consistent bound during a long active move.
- Printer::needsCMPwait returns correctionFollowsDirection, which is not the same predicate as needCmpWait or actual completion. Commands::waitForMotion, PrintPause::waitForPause and ScanProbe::moveZ depend on it.
- Printer::disableCMPnow(bool wait) disables compensation, requests zero and optionally polls correctionCanMove with delayMilliseconds(1), without the periodic pump (historical recursion avoidance). Called indirectly by Printer::setHomed whenever Z is unhomed, even for X/Y-only status changes; explicit true in RF.cpp initRF, false before replacing matrix in HeatBedScan. Queue task M3000 uses default false. homeZAxis subsequently replaces the physical reference and clears correction, so automatic unwind merely on status invalidation is an unnecessary coupling and may be unsafe. Do not equate canceling future correction with instantly moving the nozzle or forgetting physical offset.
- disableZStepper already clears scan/correction, invalidates home, lowers enable and resets reference. The candidate wake gate on unhomed requests may also prevent an old wait from completing. Remove hidden dependency before production acceptance.

## Selected architecture direction (not yet implemented)

One motion execution owner and one physical Z pulse arbiter. Use the existing Timer1 move scheduling and common acceleration/velocity primitives, with a mutable correction request alongside nominal queue/direct motion. Do not turn each 16ms target update into a normal queued G1: that would add lookahead delay, consume queue slots, change nominal Z/fade coordinates, and delay live SenseOffset. No additional PrintLine queue or global cap. Distinguish:
1. Deterministic bed-shape feedforward may be planned/interpolated ahead using the known geometry if measured benefit and exact coordinate/ECMP consistency warrant it; independent live offset remains mutable even with a full/empty queue. Do not rewrite frozen nominal moves.
2. Current effective correction target is a replaceable request, not an accumulation of stale moves. The common executor produces physically feasible correction velocity/acceleration in elapsed timer ticks, not number of primary steps, using configured Z limits and actual available timing. Reuse existing ramp math where possible; avoid float/expensive division in ISR. A correction alone is an ordinary eligible motion source at idle, not a separate HAL polling mechanism.
3. At each physical Z opportunity arbitrate nominal and correction demand together: combine opposite requests with no physical pulse while updating both appropriate ledgers; same-direction extra demand only if a later distinct legal pulse fits the total Z speed/acceleration/STEP/DIR constraints. One owner for motor pin and position, no lost or double nominal/correction step, retain limit/retreat and suppressed-overtravel policy. E/ECMP and DMS remain consistent with live Z target; no changed nominal geometry or pressure safety.
4. Remove mass/waitSteps, stepSlowedZCompensation, needCmpWait and direction-dependent needsCMPwait as legacy special cases when replacement proven. Use explicit completion/admission state for ordinary motion barriers (pause, home, scan, reference change, tool change, stop). No delay loop in setter, no wait for unreachable target or sleeping driver, no rearm after abort. Homing/reference loss cancels future correction without automatic unhomed unwind; preserve actual ledger until reference reset or a safely authorized transition. Compensation-off requests a controlled unwind if still valid and eligible; do not jump physical Z or abruptly clear positive SenseOffset while printing.
5. Existing central governor decides feasible geometry/speed jointly. If combined Z demand cannot track with bounded error, report/limit using common motion admission/governor or explicit safe fault, not ad-hoc recurring wait at queue joins. Removing legacy hold without replacing its safety/quality intent is not acceptable. Do not silently accept arbitrarily large correction lag during first layer. Distinguish safety limits from legacy pacing.

## Owner acceptance gate: Z-hop reversibility and actual DIR (2026-11-27)

Owner reports historical intermittent layer shifts after retract Z-hop on both community and Conrad firmware; many RF users avoid Z-hop. Treat as a hard regression target, **not an identified historical root cause or hardware fix claim**. Source already has balanceOpposingCorrection(): if correction and intended path are opposite and correction is permitted, account both with no pulse. Keep it. If correction toward a limit is blocked, do not consume the valid retreat. The old stepPath reads the physical DIR pin as the nominal direction; a standalone correction can change that pin. The current experimental whole-move Z reservation excludes that interleave, so it must not be relaxed before separating logical direction from actual pin.

For normal referenced, unconstrained motion, integer ledger change: delta motor = delta nominal + delta direct + delta appliedCorrection. Use applied correction, not requested target. Include reference changes, scan, suppressed queued overtravel and aborted/truncated moves as separate terms/epochs; never hide a discrepancy by rebasing on requested endpoints. At equal nominal/direct/settled correction endpoints, motor step position must close **exactly**, no tolerance of one step and no cumulative remainder. A changed settled correction target can legitimately change physical clearance at identical nominal Z. Opposite pairs consume both signed ledgers without STEP or unnecessary DIR changes; remaining net demand can be opposite to G-code sign. Emit distinct pulses only under actual physical speed/acceleration and STEP/DIR setup/hold limits; a logically canceled pair must not advance a physical ramp as though a motor pulse happened. Maintain signed physical pulse history across nominal/compensation handoffs, including short reversals and packing. Foreground may calculate/publish live targets, never be a competing normal print Z pulse writer. Homing/scan are explicit exclusive ownership epochs.

## Owner expanded acceptance: XYZ + wipe + fade transitions, feedforward (2026-11-27)

Owner explicitly requires Z-hop considered with XY travel/wipe, simultaneous XYZ, all three compensation zones (full matrix, fade, flat), rounding and reversible closure. Previous correction was perceived as lagging at high print speeds; prioritize tracking throughout the move, not merely settling at the end. Treat unknown historical failure causes as hypotheses, no hardware claim.

Source-backed geometry: for fixed valid context and z>0, B(x,y) is staged-truncated bilinear interpolation; C_geo=B below/at g_min, O+(B-O)*(g_max-z)/(g_max-g_min) inside fade, O at/above g_max. O is highest matrix value. Since B<=O, pure nominal Z within fade changes C monotonically in the SAME sign as nominal dz (away from special zero-height rule). With XY simultaneous, gradient terms can dominate and reverse net motor direction. Existing z<0 uses O+13 and z==0 uses B+13; discontinuity explicitly preserve/test, do not smooth silently. At fixed context and same represented XYZ, C_geo is a deterministic function, not accumulated incremental rounding. At same Z but different XY, changed physical offset is expected. Dynamic manual/Sense/Digit contributions remain independent mutable requests. Do not equate G-code direction, target correction and applied correction.

Current foreground samples zMotion.nominalSteps() then separate XYMotion::nominalSteps reads; latter are volatile int32 without locking on AVR. Possible torn XY or mixed XYZ epoch, not yet linked interleaving evidence. First minimal independent improvement: short coherent XYZ snapshot under a single lock, interpolation outside lock, paired correction+ECMP publication retained. Sample-age and future planned-layer context remain separate problems. 16ms watchdog update is cadence, not a latency bound; at 100mm/s already 1.6mm XY before additional foreground/step debt. Do not merely speed up correction ramp.
