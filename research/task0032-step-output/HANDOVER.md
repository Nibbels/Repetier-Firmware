## B — rejected / not selected

* Raw ECMP speedup violates physical minimum E-low. Keep minimal verified guards; never trade electrical margin for cycles.
* Float Quake inverse root: median694 cycles vs HAL integer root313; float period839, exact float sqrt/div986, integer direct period301. No blanket approximate root replacement; raw floating approximation not a safe bound.
* Legacy correction coupled to primary-step/idle cadence: speed-dependent lag and pure-E starvation. Do not solve with arbitrary higher mass or additional waits. Fixed correction-gap threshold2048 removes observed synthetic late releases but increases tracking time and work; no production threshold selected.
* Unconditional n=1 Timer1, pure packing elimination, random jitter and global speed or microstep changes. Keep smallest feasible deterministic grouping; distribute physical events only with a measured amortized ramp/dispatch architecture and whole-system budget.
* Naive single endpoint chord across cells/fade, independent rounding of nominal and geometry, silently smoothing the historical z=0 discontinuity, accepting endpoint closure alone, or deleting a real opposing-pair ledger contribution. Reject as correctness policy.
* Two-slot streaming without admission, simply increasing queue capacity, or adding per-MoveQueue-slot geometry storage without A73 evidence. A bigger buffer cannot fix sustained preparation cost. Central already selected 24 PrintLine slots.
* Unconditional positive-E layer confirmation and using physical base E steps after flow/Advance as slicer retraction intent are not sufficient. The proposed logical E classification is evidence, not an adopted production rule.
* Whole-register shadow / locking all fastio, full LCD frame, entire DRV frame, or compensating only on the consumer side for GPIO lost updates: not justified by audited ownership.
