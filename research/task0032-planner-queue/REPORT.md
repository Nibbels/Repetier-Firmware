## Initial source audit
MoveQueue::remainingMotion snapshots count/read index/front remaining steps under IRQ protection, then scans published slots with interrupts enabled. Only foreground can reuse slots during this foreground call. It ignores warmup and task flags, but NOT blocked profiles, pause, direct moves, Advance drain, compensation or dispatch latency. Its time is an analytic ramp estimate, not exact wall time. XYZ/E diagnostic path needs a square root and floating point geometry per movement, additional up to three roots for ramp time. Never call this in an ISR.
MotionPlanner::prepareMove stores timeInTicks AFTER paceShortMove but BEFORE axis/interval limiting. This is neither original requested time nor final planned time. The original feedrate can be recovered only at admission, not generally from the retained slots. Primary acceleration is integer-bound by secondary-axis steps, including rare 64-bit division path. Preserve this guarantee. The low-depth rule (<10 entries, <250000 ticks) has a discontinuity at count 10, and may slow an empty-queue short move even without a resource constraint.
updateLookahead skips front even if not active, then reserves following entries until sum(timeInTicks) >=45000 ticks (24+ slots); searches backward to first eligible after a fixed exit. It sets firstMove.block() under IRQ lock, runs replanning unlocked, then publishes each recalculated step profile and unblocks. Count of examined reserved/fixed entries matters for interrupt mask time. Fixed joins and conservative exits can shrink mutable horizon independently of queue depth. E-only/non-E-only or Advance E/non-E boundaries keep original safe entry/exit instead of normal junction planning. Never use total scheduled time as mutable braking distance. No permission to rewrite active or reserved moves.
limitJunction uses dot-product angular metric 1-cos weighted by (0.6/previous.distance)^2 below 0.6mm; asymmetric for unequal chords, floor at half XY jerk before independent Z/E delta checks. Revisit only with actual-source counterexamples, quantized zero-E and Advance transitions. No silent policy change.
planDeceleration and planAcceleration traverse the mutable suffix; each iteration may sqrt, update flags and invalidate profiles; prepareStepProfile adds integer divisions and ramp fitting. A per-command constant CPU cost is not justified. Need counts by path and linked AVR cycles including mask section, not host ns translated to AVR.

## Recommended preliminary telemetry separation
1. Nominal requested work: sum distance/requested speed at admission; optional independent aggregate. Existing timeInTicks is a paced-request proxy, not original request. Avoid extra per-slot bytes if proxy sufficient; no ISR floats.
2. Scheduled lower proxy: sum primary remaining steps / min(vMax,31250) over motion slots; compare against current analytic reference. Includes ramp underestimation and is not guaranteed executable time. Candidate cache and tangent alternatives from parent queue-estimate.cjs must be scored on real profiles including triangular ramps and active tail.
3. Contiguous executable reserve: stop at first blocked/unready/task/warmup; explicitly gate on pause/direct/executor state (queue alone cannot guarantee motion). Do not count behind barrier. Retain status bit/reason and age. May use same lower proxy; not safety deadline.
4. Mutable horizon: compute first eligible exactly as updateLookahead, record first slot and suffix distance, incoming fixed speed and per-move acceleration constraints. Separate remaining time from braking distance. A fully stocked queue can have zero published mutable slots. Recompute at admission, not on timer.
Suggested first candidate: foreground-only O(N) scan, no per-slot storage, fixed-point/tick estimate only if measured win. If necessary evaluate aggregate maintained outside ISR with bounded retire notification; not design governor. M3200 P14 exposes XYZ, E and seconds; cannot remove diagnostic before central owner checks compatibility and replacements. Separate telemetry fast path from existing reporting initially.

## Braking analytic example (not a controller)
At 40 bytes per command, 20k B/s corresponds to 500 commands/s; sustained .025/.05/.1/.2/.5 mm path means 12.5/25/50/100/250 mm/s respectively. These are supply examples, not individual caps. Braking from 100 to 25 mm/s requires (100^2-25^2)/(2*a) mm; at illustrative 1000 mm/s², 4.6875 mm. Even 32 short .05mm moves provide at most 1.6mm before reserve/fixed entries and cannot guarantee that transition. Long earlier mutable segment may provide distance; if already frozen, no hindsight. Test externally supplied caps before/after freeze; no hidden knowledge of future geometry. Acceleration is an upper bound; avoid needless oscillation. Long independent travel does not need steady command supply.

## Historical rule simplification candidates (SOURCE/ANALYTIC, not accepted changes)
- Replace length-factor computation with a normalized-direction or curvature rule only after comparing quantized short segments, unequal adjacent lengths and near reversal. For a constant-radius path, the existing (1-cos(theta))/Lprev² tends to 1/(2R²) at small equal chords; deleting the length weight would change physical behavior with slicer resolution. A 0.05mm chord on a 1mm radius gives approximately 0.18 for the weighted dimensionless term (0.6mm normalization), vs 0.00125 unweighted. The same small chord with a single quantized lateral step can instead produce a much larger angle. Avoid fitting simplifications to ideal unquantized circles.
- Existing asymmetric length factor is especially suspect at long→short→long and unequal chords. A symmetric geometry candidate is appropriate for later replay, not accepted here. Preserve independent Z/E velocity-change checks and separate E-only/Advance joins. Their speed-vector differences are currently calculated from each segment's own full speed, not normalized to the actual junction speed. Reducing the full-speed limit can therefore alter a junction even with unchanged geometry.
- Forward/backward reachability uses squared speeds and distance; compare squared values before sqrt when possible, but preserve minSpeed floors, fixed flags, profile invalidation, and conservative exit behavior. A change to fixed-join rules changes which older movements are mutable; not just a math optimization. Need variant replay before any production simplification.
- Remove redundant calculations first (time-only estimator and proven exact arithmetic transformations); no removal of user diagnostic M3200 P14, acceleration checks, pause/direct barriers or Advance transitions on present evidence.

## Current decision input for parent 0032 (not a governor)
Provide nominal motion work at admission, planned duration on a bounded cadence or validated cache, immediately executable contiguous reserve with barrier/validity flag, and mutable lookahead boundary plus distance/entry speed/available acceleration. Do not equate any two of these. Planning cost should expose number of replanned slots and bounded mask window; admission counts alone do not characterize CPU demand. Keep existing diagnostic callable on demand while evaluating a cheap foreground signal; pacedTicks is cheap but underestimates planned time by ~45% mean, cannot be sole safety/control input. Exact-time-only no-storage variant is useful reference, not yet optimal periodic implementation. Preserve high-speed isolated travel. Use early externally supplied caps only where actual mutable geometry permits, not per-command clamp from wire rate. Current evidence gives no justification for increasing 24 slots or removing historical junction/Advance safeguards wholesale. Follow-up needed: improve exact/ramp-cache tradeoff, discriminate arrival-limited cases (less prefill and longer stalls), replay alternative junction math and classify entry/exit mismatch, actual integrated CPU budget after P0 and A73.
