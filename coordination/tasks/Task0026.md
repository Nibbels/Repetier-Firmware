### Task0027 handover: existing Timer1 defect

Both baseline and candidate linked on all four boards reproduce a pre-existing low-byte error in TIMER1_COMPA wait branch. For reachable delay 0x11abc, setTimer subtracts 0x8000 and leaves stepperWait=0x9abc. When high byte nonzero, brne last skips lds r22,stepperWait; OCR1A becomes 0x9a00/0x9abc/0x9aff for interrupted r22=0/0xbc/0xff, rather than always 0x9abc. Remainder is cleared. Low-only branch also lacks tst r22 after lds (normal chunking cannot create low-only nonzero remainder). Fix in Task0027 with timer boundary traces, guard late compare and prove reachable ranges; do not change packing silently. This issue was not introduced by specialization.
