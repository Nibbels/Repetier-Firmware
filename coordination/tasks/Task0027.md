### Timer resources and packing-policy decision

Timer0 core timekeeping OVF (/64), COMPA Advance E on-demand, COMPB software heater/fan PWM, ADC and periodic service (64 counts). Timer1 COMPA CTC /1 XYZ/base E, compare A owns TOP; other compares cannot independently schedule across TOP reset without new design. Timer4 hardware RGB fast PWM /8 ICR4=20480 where configured (RF2000 family PH3/4/5 OC4A/B/C); RF1000 PH5 bed heater mapping prevents assuming universal RGB/PWM pin availability. Timer2 OC2A/B PB4/PH6 wired hotend heater outputs, currently software PWM, do not repurpose. Timer3 OC3B/C PE4/5 are RF1000 Y/X min switch inputs; Timer5 OC5A PL3 RF1000 Z STEP. Timer2/3/5 no active linked ISR (non-weak vector inventory checked), Arduino initialization not proof of unused physical pin. WDT separate compensation/heartbeat, ADC completion polling in COMPB, USART vectors independent. No timer/pin/channel reassignment; no hardware heater PWM experiment because independent fail-off contract and board mapping not verified.
