### Retained decisions, throughput and acceptance boundary

Task0023: keep -Os/LTO. O3 does not fit; O2 did not establish broad safe advantage; mrelax small layout-dependent improvement; no-LTO slow wait improvement did not justify hot-path/size trade. Task0024 retained only safe compression/cold storage, Task0025 exact lookup (+25914 Flash) trades program space for deterministic math, Task0026 specialized XY, Task0027 narrow packing and Timer1 high-byte wait correction (206613 wait cases and 8448867 packing comparisons in host replay), Task0028 receiver budget 127 bytes and ~15 cycles/ordinary byte, Task0029 allocation-free RAM diagnostic (only setup/diagnostic, not constant print-loop saving). Keep 24 slots, 115200 baud, existing lookahead, minimum interval and packing threshold 3300..5000. No speculative increases in throughput or baud.

Dense short move model unchanged: 0.20 mm, group 1/32/96 -> 39.73/39.80/41.16 mm/s; group 96 has 45.47 ms empty gap. 0.60 mm all groups 75.15 mm/s. These are host model results, not measured printer speeds. Nominal 115200 8N1 yields 11520 B/s, about 288 40-byte lines/s, limiting 0.2 mm chord stream to 57.6 mm/s absent other effects. Real protocol overhead and buffering change the result. No pacing change made.
