## Confirmed starting point

At f5b86f20f2636228365509448bb9366cf0f69ad4 (docs only since Task0030): HAL.cpp Timer0 COMPB increments PWM counter every nominal 256us, heater frame 32.768ms (128 ticks), latches requestedDuty at start; duty max 254 holds HIGH across frames. A zero demand is not itself an immediate pin write in HAL::setHeaterDuty. Interrupt blackout can stretch HIGH; foreground stall may repeat stale duty indefinitely until watchdog/reset or other protection. Commands::checkForPeriodicalActions renews generic heartbeat on scan tick before manageTemperatures. WDT is interrupt-only, nominal 16ms; pings external WDI using millis age < WATCHDOG_MAIN_LOOP_TIMEOUT (20s), and posts compensation tick. A generic heartbeat is not proof of thermal completion; timebase may lose overflows with IRQ masked. TemperatureControl.cpp normal PID publishes duty per controller, autotune owns demand and can hold it across many loops; explicit renewal after valid completed thermal checks is required for both. Fault latching, zero target, sensor invalid/stale, autotune exit, boot, reset and all other heater pin writers must be inventoried. Do not assert historical crash cause.

## Requirements and architecture options

1. Separate requested duty, fresh valid thermal authorization, physical pulse state and actual delivered energy. New pulses require fresh completed sensor and fault checks; no generic scan heartbeat or repeated ISR rearm of stale authorization. Expiry absolute and wrap-safe with bounded clock progress. Fail closed on missing, stale, invalid or inconsistent state; zero/inhibit must cancel pending/active pulses immediately where CPU running. Fault/timeout must not automatically resume heating on returning foreground after a prolonged stall; latch/recovery policy explicit. All channels independent where useful, global fault stops all. No pulse restart without fresh permission. Preserve normal 100ms controller rate; derive lease and maximum pulse from actual worst healthy service, thermal power/risk and timer resolution, not arbitrary constants. Model full duty as bounded authorization, not perpetual HIGH.
2. Prefer interrupt-independent hardware falling edge (non-PWM compare clear, explicit rearm after checks; verify OC latch setup/COM switching, counter wrap, register update races, actual pin polarity, reset and timer mode). Hotend0 PB4 OC2A, hotend1 PH6 OC2B, RF1000 bed PH5 OC4C; RF2000/v2 bed PE2 has NO OC. Timer2 shared counter, compare priority above Timer1, 8bit /1024 max 16.384ms span; normal mode and independent compare channels need bounded pulse scheduling. RF1000 Timer4 routing differs from RF2000 RGB. No unsafe timer remap or board mods. Hardware auto-clear only while clock and pin peripheral function operate. Generic free-running PWM repeats stale duty and is not a fail-safe. For non-OC bed investigate short dedicated off ISR plus independent hardware reset/gate; no software IRQ alone guarantees off with I=0 or CPU stall. Investigate AVR WDT reset mode/interrupt+reset and external TPS3820-50 wiring/revision/power-stage default-off (published RF2000 WDI PC0 vs firmware PA7 discrepancy). Never continuously feed WDT from mere ISR or generic loop; proof thermal progress, and actual reset drives power stage off including bootloader. Independent physical overtemperature/power cut needed for stuck switch, clock failure, etc. No claim universal guarantee from firmware.
3. Separate heater edge scheduling from ADC, fan, LCD and ticks; compare event-driven frame start + falling edges versus current software counting, preserve average duty/period initially. Timer2 priority useful when IRQs enabled, not preemption or stall immunity. Compare unchanged architecture with explicit lease first, then bounded hardware pulse candidates; no requirement to remove Advance or other IRQs. Consider staggered pulses and safe quantization only after checking power-stage topology, thermal dynamics and PID/autotune delivered duty. Owner notes energy averaging: 10/25/40% over equal periods equals 25% average. Do NOT compensate lost heating across fault or expiry, or allow unbounded energy debt/catch-up. Optional bounded normal-mode quantization correction only with fresh authorization; preserve max power and temperature safety.
4. Reproducible fault model before production changes: normal duty 0..255, full demand, foreground dead while IRQs run, IRQ disabled while HIGH, overload with delayed OFF/ON, timer flag coalescence, stale ADC, valid heartbeat but no completed PID, sensor disconnect/short, stop/inhibit, autotune stuck and exit, reset/brownout/boot, timer wrap, multiple heater channels, renewed/revoked permission race. Distinguish hardware OC clear, ISR-only clear, reset gate assumption. Record actual heater HIGH duration, cumulative energy and expiry/last fresh check to physical-off latency; unsupported physical cases not passes. No energy compensation after failure. Verify normal profiles and timing/CPU cost with equal load and 500k UART stress, no regression to motion pulses or critical service deadlines.
