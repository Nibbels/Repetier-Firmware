## Optional final review

Inspect the final firmware's authoritative time, PrintJob, motion/extrusion, persistence and reporting boundaries. Decide whether usage statistics have sufficient practical benefit relative to their complexity, static SRAM, stack, CPU/IRQ cost, EEPROM wear and recovery risk. The default is omission; a documented decision not to implement is a successful completion. Do not invent a counter just to close this task.

If the owner later explicitly elects to add statistics, propose semantics before implementation: distinguish actual powered-on time, active print-job time, heater usage and physically commanded filament motion; account for cancellation, manual filament handling, tool changes, retract/unretract, Advance/ECMP, time rollover, reset and power-loss policy. A commanded motor step is not independent measurement of delivered filament. Reuse existing authoritative central interfaces without duplicated drive/timing or position accounting. No additional hot-path floating point or unqualified per-pulse overhead, no synchronous EEPROM access from the ISR, no wear-heavy settings transactions or safety dependency on optional statistics. Evaluate read-only host/LCD reporting only if wanted. Do not reserve or reuse EEPROM addresses before approved design and validation.
