### Ownership and compatibility decisions

- The supported target is exclusively ATmega2560. `BoardConfiguration.h` composes user options, factory model profile, override defaults and `pins.h`. HAL declares this dependency directly. `Configuration.h` remains the editable common policy/default interface; RF1000/RF2000 model profiles remain factory defaults. Physical I2C addresses (0x49, 0x50, 0x51) move to the pin-map owner with their exact values. No connector or retrofit guard removed.
- Move RGB mode/status values to `RgbLighting.h`, manual move mode values to `ManualMotion.h`, and temperature ADC channel index to HAL; preserve values and order. Move math helper into `RMath.h`; keep Arduino min/max handling. Add direct dependencies to Commands, RF, Extruder, UI, Communication, Printer, motion and MoveQueue headers; Communication no longer needs the firmware umbrella in its translation unit. Keep the umbrella as compatibility entry point for remaining callers while auditing their concrete dependencies.
- Remove `UI_PRINTER_COMPANY` and `VIRTUAL_AXIS` (Task0004 source and compiler audit), unreferenced axisNames declaration and duplicate drv8711Init declaration. Remove guards around MUX5 in the HAL for the only supported MCU and redundant MOTHERBOARD guard around the watchdog diagnostic; retain watchdog error and all board, single/dual extruder and connector/retrofit conditions. Actual baseline and candidate AVR preprocessing is required before accepting these removals.
- No change to EEPROM layout, temperature calibration, G-code dispatch, panel actions or persistence semantics. Host communication/EEPROM adapters strip the explicit HAL include because they model that dependency; golden assertions unchanged.
- `coordination/audits/Task0015-preprocessor.py` replays the actual Arduino compiler database in all four builds against the candidate and a git-archived target, emits per-unit include trees and checks observed removed macros and board definitions. The task-specific workflow step is temporary, not intended as a hardcoded ongoing CI policy. Restore the shared workflow after the audit is captured and verify final inputs.
