### Explicit owner decision on STEP timing (2026-11-25)

Owner extends Task0028 scope to HAL::stepWithCount() and STEP timing documentation, overriding the previous conservative 32-cycle recommendation: **31 CPU cycles at 16 MHz is the production standard for the explicitly timed Advance HIGH phase**. Round the DRV8711 minimum 1.9 us upward to whole cycles: 31 = 1.9375 us, nominal 37.5 ns margin; 32 = 2 us. Preserve LOW phase and DIR setup/hold, normal XYZ/E pulse work and accounting. Inspect actual linked edges for all four variants and both tools. Theoretical saving is one CPU cycle per emitted Advance pulse, about 0.104% CPU at 16666.7 pulses/s, not 3.125% overall. Below 31 cycles is an experimental optimization candidate only: 30 = 1.875 us, 25 ns below specification; unknown manufacturer margin cannot justify production use. No smaller-pulse experiment or physical flash authorized in this task. Separate future instrumented driver-level tests across voltage, temperature and device variation, count accepted steps and measure pin edges; even successful samples do not establish datasheet compliance. No production change below 31 without a separate explicit decision.
