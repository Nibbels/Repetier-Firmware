### Advance HIGH phase

Linked COMPA disassembly on each board and each fitted tool (six pulse paths total): E0 PORTA bit 4, E1 PORTC bit 1. HIGH interval measured by counting instructions between completed SBI and completed CBI: two stores 4 cycles, LDI 1, eight DEC 8, seven taken BRNE 14 plus last untaken BRNE 1, one NOP 1, CBI 2 = 31 cycles (formerly two NOPs/32). At nominal 16 MHz this is 1.9375 us. Full vector comparisons using symbol-size bounds and normalized relocation/branch destinations show exactly one removed NOP per fitted tool and no other instruction/control-flow differences; WDT, Timer1 COMPA, Timer0 COMPB/OVF, UART RX/UDRE vectors identical on all four. The change saves one cycle on the pulse path, not on non-pulse paths. Existing compare cadence, DIR setup and output accounting remain; no physical pin or accepted-step measurements, no smaller-pulse experiment. Owner's explicit 31-cycle production decision documented in HAL.h and docs/motion-timing.md; below-minimum experiments separate.
