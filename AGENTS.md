# Firmware maintenance handover

**Development roadmap:** [Architecture and development blocks](docs/development-roadmap.md). The active block is coordinate systems and motion compensation. The [maintenance list](docs/maintenance-tasks.md) tracks its fixes and later work. Evaluate step packing in the motion smoothness and performance block. Review optional features before migrating their code. [Milling mode has been retired](docs/development-roadmap.md#feature-retirement).

**Verified coordinate semantics:** [coordinate frames and extrusion timeline](docs/first-layer-control/coordinate-frames-and-extrusion.md). `g_min/g_max` refer to nominal planned Z steps (not compensated motor position). The [M3912 patch 5f848c9](https://github.com/Nibbels/Repetier-Firmware/commit/5f848c9a5cd0347c6d45fd9d3f13afd7344cb775) now restores the incoming *logical* E coordinate. The user M220 speed override and DMS `dynamicFeedrateFactor`/`interval_mod` are independent. Instantaneous Digit-Z differs from accumulated SenseOffset.

**M3912 uses an isolated pressure-braked priming phase.** `g_startLineActive` identifies generated prime moves. High DMS force progressively constrains XY speed and reduces E flow, like the audible change when a chainsaw enters wood. Manual start code enables SenseOffset with `M3909` after priming. [State ledger and test plan](docs/first-layer-control/m3912-state-boundary.md)

**Start with [docs/first-layer-control/README.md](docs/first-layer-control/README.md) before editing first-layer, Z compensation, DMS or priming behavior. For the full Z-offset sum, boot/print lifecycle, matrix EEPROM and thermal/scanning risks, read [docs/z-offset/README.md](docs/z-offset/README.md).** The linked documentation explains original intent, actual execution paths, risks and test scenarios.

This is shared AVR/ATmega2560 Repetier firmware for **RF1000, RF2000 and RF2000v2**. Motion planning, SenseOffset and bed correction are common code. Do not apply model-only changes without a demonstrated model-specific cause. Stepper timing and SRAM/flash limits matter; avoid expensive arithmetic in ISRs.

**Design essentials.** DMS measures strain-gauge force. Excess force may indicate inadequate nozzle clearance, a clog or material, temperature or speed differences. SenseOffset (`M3909`) adds bounded positive clearance on the first layer. Bed-profile compensation follows the scanned bed, then fades its XY variation across later layers with geometric E-material compensation (ECMP). `M3912` primes at nominal Z=0.35 mm and suspends layer learning and SenseOffset for the duration of the line. The dynamically learned `g_minZCompensationSteps` controls both the start of profile fade and eligibility for SenseOffset.

**Non-negotiable operator workflow:** In Mod Menu page 2 the Z/bed DOWN→UP button pair intentionally transfers 5 µm of live SenseOffset into EEPROM-backed manual `Printer::ZOffset` with approximately unchanged final clearance (see [live takeover procedure](docs/z-offset/history-and-workflows.md) and the original January 2017 changelog). Do not “fix” the asymmetric button behavior or reset dynamic offsets while a manual transfer is underway.

**Lifecycle fix implemented:** Z homing safely restores min/max, clears the three layer markers and volatile SenseOffset, preserving the manual EEPROM offset ([ba93708](https://github.com/Nibbels/Repetier-Firmware/commit/ba93708d41c84fcce16872720b6b954da70ea157)). Compensation-off cleanup now clears any remaining dynamic correction even after `M3909 P0`, plus pressure history ([733833a](https://github.com/Nibbels/Repetier-Firmware/commit/733833a7c062b461c9fbb9de8a72ea202a65c812)). **Prints started without Z homing are not fully fixed**. Inspect [test scenarios / hypotheses](docs/first-layer-control/test-scenarios.md) before modifying reset behavior. A fork's RF2000v2-only post-priming workaround is not justified for this shared planner.

**Git workflow:** `ai-firmware-review` is the isolated working branch; keep `community_development` unchanged until specifically instructed. The DRV8711 BLANK invalid read command was removed without changing actual default electrical timings in [commit 8f00ed8](https://github.com/Nibbels/Repetier-Firmware/commit/8f00ed8df6a0e825dee368c1233ffc4934349faa).
